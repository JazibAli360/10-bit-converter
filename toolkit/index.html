What this does

This converts 8-bit AI-generated video to 10-bit. It does three honest things:

  • Kills banding — the visible stepping in skies, smoke, and gradients (deband + dither).
  • Adds grading headroom — a true 10-bit intermediate so pushing curves in DaVinci/Premiere doesn't re-introduce banding.
  • Optionally 16-bit precision — cleaner gradient math, quantized to 10-bit. Precision only.

What it does NOT do: it can't recover detail that was never generated, and it uses no AI — nothing reinterprets, invents, or removes your detail and texture. If footage looks flat rather than banded, that's a generation-quality issue, not something a bit-depth conversion can fix.

Tip: use Preview scopes or Compare to see the effect before committing to a full encode.
